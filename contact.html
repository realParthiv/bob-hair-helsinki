BOB Hair Helsinki: The Vision

Born from a radical idea, BOB Hair Helsinki redefines the salon experience. We champion the power of the haircut as a statement, echoing the defiant spirit of the original bob that shattered conventions in the 1920s. Our philosophy is simple: beauty is an art form, a cultural expression, and a catalyst for change.

We are a nexus where hairdressing, art, fashion, and design converge, creating a vibrant cultural hub in the heart of Helsinki.
