A New Era of Hairdressing

BOB is more than a salon. It was born from the belief that a haircut can be a revolutionary act — just like the original bob cut of the 1920s challenged every social norm of its time. We are a space where hairdressing meets art, where clients come not just for a cut but for an experience. Our walls rotate local Helsinki artists. Our team collaborates with fashion designers and photographers. We believe beauty is culture, and culture shapes beauty. Vidal Sassoon said it best — if you don't look good, we don't look good. That philosophy drives everything we do.

Vidal Sassoon Partnership

We are proud to be a Vidal Sassoon partner salon. This esteemed affiliation signifies our commitment to the highest standards of hairdressing, precision cutting, and innovative styling that have defined Vidal Sassoon's legendary approach to beauty. Our team continually refines their skills through exclusive training, ensuring that every client receives an unparalleled salon experience rooted in excellence and artistry.
